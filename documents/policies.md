# Company Policies

## Delivery Policy

Standard deliveries are completed within 3–7 business days depending on the destination.

## Return Policy

Customers may request returns within seven days of delivery for eligible products.

## Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled before shipment dispatch.

## Damaged Shipment Policy

Damaged shipments should be reported within 24 hours of delivery.

## Customer Support

Customer support is available during business hours to resolve shipment and delivery-related issues.
