# Warehouse Operations

## Overview

Warehouse operations ensure that inventory is stored, managed, and dispatched efficiently. Proper warehouse management improves delivery speed, reduces operational costs, and minimizes inventory errors.

## Major Warehouse Activities

### Receiving

Incoming products are unloaded and inspected for quality and quantity.

### Storage

Products are stored in designated locations based on category and demand.

### Inventory Management

Inventory levels are monitored continuously to maintain product availability.

### Picking

Warehouse employees collect products based on customer orders.

### Packing

Products are securely packaged to prevent damage during transportation.

### Dispatch

Completed shipments are transferred to transportation partners for delivery.

## Importance

Efficient warehouse operations improve customer satisfaction, reduce shipment delays, and support accurate order fulfillment.
