# Logistics Terminology

## Warehouse

A facility where products are stored before shipment.

## Inventory

The total quantity of products available in storage.

## Shipment

A package prepared for transportation.

## Dispatch

The process of sending shipments from the warehouse.

## Carrier

The transportation company responsible for moving shipments.

## Last-Mile Delivery

The final stage of delivery from the destination hub to the customer's address.

## Fulfillment

The complete process of receiving, processing, packing, and delivering customer orders.

## Tracking Number

A unique identifier used to monitor shipment progress throughout the logistics network.
