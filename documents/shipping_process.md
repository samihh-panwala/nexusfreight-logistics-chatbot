# Shipping Process

## Overview

The shipping process begins after customer orders have been packed and prepared for transportation.

## Shipping Stages

- Shipment Created
- Ready for Dispatch
- Dispatched
- In Transit
- At Destination Hub
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered

## Tracking

Each shipment receives a unique Shipment ID that allows customers and logistics staff to monitor delivery progress in real time.

## Transportation

Shipments may pass through multiple logistics hubs before reaching the destination city. At every checkpoint, the shipment status is updated in the tracking system.

## Completion

The shipping process ends when the customer successfully receives the package and the delivery status changes to "Delivered."
