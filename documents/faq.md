# Frequently Asked Questions

## How can I track my order?

Customers can track orders using the Order ID or Shipment ID.

## How long does delivery take?

Most deliveries are completed within three to seven business days.

## Can I cancel my order?

Orders may be cancelled before they are dispatched.

## What does "Out for Delivery" mean?

The shipment has been assigned to a delivery executive and is expected to arrive soon.

## What should I do if my shipment is delayed?

Customers can check the tracking page or contact customer support for assistance.
