# Shipment Status Definitions

## Order Confirmed

The order has been successfully received and verified.

## Packed

The products have been packed and are ready for shipment.

## Shipment Created

A shipment record has been generated.

## Dispatched

The shipment has left the warehouse.

## In Transit

The shipment is moving between logistics hubs.

## At Destination Hub

The shipment has reached the warehouse nearest to the customer.

## Out for Delivery

The shipment has been assigned to a delivery executive and is on the way.

## Delivered

The shipment has been successfully delivered to the customer.
