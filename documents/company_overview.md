# Company Overview

## Introduction

Logicrest Logistics is a technology-driven logistics and supply chain company that provides reliable transportation, warehousing, inventory management, and last-mile delivery services across India. The company focuses on delivering goods safely and efficiently while maintaining transparency throughout the shipment lifecycle.

## Core Services

- Warehousing and Inventory Management
- Order Processing and Fulfillment
- Domestic Transportation
- Shipment Tracking
- Last-Mile Delivery
- Customer Support Services

## Mission

To simplify logistics by providing reliable, fast, and technology-enabled delivery solutions that improve customer satisfaction and operational efficiency.

## Vision

To become one of India's leading logistics service providers by integrating artificial intelligence, automation, and real-time tracking into supply chain operations.

## Business Operations

The company manages customer orders through a centralized logistics platform. Every shipment is assigned a unique Order ID and Shipment ID, allowing customers and employees to track deliveries in real time.

Warehouses located in multiple cities help reduce delivery time and improve inventory management. Delivery executives are assigned shipments based on location, availability, and optimized delivery routes.

## Customer Support

Customers can monitor shipments, report delivery issues, request assistance, and receive shipment updates through the logistics support system.
