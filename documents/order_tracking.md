# Order Tracking

## Overview

Order tracking enables customers and company staff to monitor shipment progress throughout the delivery process.

## Tracking Information

Customers can track their orders using:

- Order ID
- Shipment ID

## Common Tracking Status

- Order Confirmed
- Packed
- Shipment Created
- Dispatched
- In Transit
- At Destination Hub
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered

Tracking information is updated automatically whenever a shipment reaches a new logistics checkpoint.
