# Delivery Process

## Overview

The delivery process represents the final stage of the logistics workflow, where shipments are transported from the destination hub to the customer's address.

## Process

1. Shipment arrives at the destination hub.
2. A delivery executive is assigned.
3. Delivery routes are optimized.
4. The shipment is marked as "Out for Delivery."
5. The package is delivered to the customer.
6. Delivery confirmation is recorded in the system.

## Failed Deliveries

If the customer is unavailable, the delivery may be rescheduled according to company policies.

## Benefits

A well-organized delivery process ensures timely deliveries and improves customer satisfaction.
