# Logistics Workflow

## Overview

The logistics workflow defines the complete sequence of activities involved in moving an order from the customer to its final destination.

## Workflow Steps

### 1. Order Placement

The customer places an order through the online platform.

### 2. Order Verification

The system verifies payment, product availability, and delivery address.

### 3. Inventory Check

The warehouse confirms that the requested products are available.

### 4. Picking

Warehouse staff collect the ordered products from storage.

### 5. Packing

Products are securely packed and labeled for transportation.

### 6. Shipment Creation

A Shipment ID is generated and assigned to the order.

### 7. Dispatch

The shipment leaves the warehouse and is handed over to the transportation carrier.

### 8. Transportation

The shipment moves through one or more logistics hubs before reaching the destination city.

### 9. Last-Mile Delivery

The delivery executive collects the shipment from the destination hub and delivers it to the customer.

### 10. Delivery Confirmation

The customer receives the package and the delivery status is updated to "Delivered."

Every stage updates the shipment tracking system so customers can monitor the progress of their orders.
